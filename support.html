Full privacy policy draft

Getting started

No account is needed. Create your first personal profile, then add medical events, appointments and documents. Choose English, Hebrew or System Default in Settings → Language. The free tier supports one new profile.

How does Full Unlock work?

Full Unlock is an optional one-time non-consumable Apple purchase, with no subscription. It enables unlimited family members, relationships, a family-wide agenda, encrypted profile sharing and readable medical-record exports. Existing medical information stays accessible; full encrypted backup and restore remain free. Apple displays the current local price.

Restore Purchases

Open Settings → Full Unlock → Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account associated with the purchase; connectivity or Apple authentication may be required. This restores verified purchase access, not medical records. Medical backups do not contain purchase ownership. If Apple cannot verify a purchase, check the account and connectivity and try again.

Where is my information stored?

Medical records and documents are stored privately on this device. There is no developer-operated medical cloud or automatic cross-device sync. Sensitive files are excluded from ordinary OS app backup. Support cannot view or recover local records remotely.

Create an encrypted backup

Open Settings → Backup & Export and choose the full encrypted backup action. Choose a strong password and follow the app’s prompts. After creation, use Save or share backup to save a copy outside this device, then confirm the external file is available. Creating or checking a backup alone does not establish an external recovery copy. Keep the password safely and separately.

Restore an encrypted backup

In Settings → Backup & Export, choose restoration/import, select your saved full backup and enter its password. Review the summary and the replacement warning before confirming. Full restore replaces all current local family and medical data. Make a separate current backup first if needed. The destination’s App Lock and language settings remain local; Calendar links and purchase ownership are not transferred.

Forgotten passwords, device loss and uninstalling

The app does not retain backup passwords and the developer cannot reset or recover them. Without an external backup and its password, loss, damage or uninstalling may permanently lose records. A backup kept only inside the app is not device-loss recovery.

Camera, documents and permissions

For scanning, allow camera access in iOS Settings for Medical Timeline. If denied, reopen Settings to review permissions; you can import an existing document instead. Native camera capture is unavailable in Simulator. If a file from a cloud-backed Files provider is unavailable, download it in Files and select it again. That provider operates under its own policies. Imported originals can retain metadata. OCR happens on-device and accuracy and language support vary.

Reminders, Calendar and sharing

Review iOS notification permissions and device settings if reminders do not appear, and reopen the app to reconcile upcoming reminders. Calendar export is optional and uses generic titles. Calendar accounts and chosen sharing/printing destinations may store or sync their copies outside the app. Readable PDF/ZIP exports are not password-encrypted by the app and are not full restoration backups.

Report a technical problem safely

Email the developer with the app version from Settings → About, iOS version, iPhone model, what you tried, what happened and reproducible steps using fictional examples. Do not send medical documents, patient names, family photos, private backups, passwords or screenshots containing health information. Describe the control or error category without including private content. Support provides app assistance, not clinical advice or emergency care.
