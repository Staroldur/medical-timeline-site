Full privacy policy

Getting started

No account is needed. Create your first personal profile, then add medical events, appointments and documents. Choose English, Hebrew or System Default in Settings → Language. The free tier supports one new profile.

How does Full Unlock work?

Full Unlock is an optional one-time non-consumable Apple purchase, with no subscription. It enables unlimited family members, relationships, a family-wide agenda, individual medical profile sharing and readable medical-record exports. Existing medical information stays accessible; full backup and restore remain free. Apple displays the current local price.

Restore Purchases

Open Settings → Full Unlock → Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account associated with the purchase; connectivity or Apple authentication may be required. This restores verified purchase access, not medical records. Medical backups do not contain purchase ownership. If Apple cannot verify a purchase, check the account and connectivity and try again.

Where is my information stored?

Medical records and documents are stored privately on this device. There is no developer-operated medical cloud or automatic cross-device sync. Sensitive files are excluded from ordinary OS app backup. Support cannot view or recover local records remotely.

Create and save a backup

Open Settings → Backup & Export → Create backup. Follow the prompts, then choose Files or a sharing app to save the .medtimeline file. Keep it in a trusted location outside this device, and check that the saved file is available. No password is required.

Restore a backup

Open Settings → Backup & Export → Restore backup or import profile and select your saved full .medtimeline backup. Review the summary and choose Replace current data when you are ready. Full restore replaces all current family and medical records, so save a separate current backup first if needed. App Lock and language settings stay local; Calendar links and purchase ownership are not transferred.

Files from older pre-release versions cannot be opened by this version. Keep the old file and create a new backup from the original device after updating the app.

Device loss and uninstalling

Without an external backup, loss, damage or uninstalling may permanently lose records. A backup kept only inside the app is not device-loss recovery.

Camera, documents and permissions

For scanning, allow camera access in iOS Settings for Medical Timeline. If denied, reopen Settings to review permissions; you can import an existing document instead. Native camera capture is unavailable in Simulator. If a file from a cloud-backed Files provider is unavailable, download it in Files and select it again. That provider operates under its own policies. Imported originals can retain metadata. OCR happens on-device and accuracy and language support vary.

Reminders, Calendar and sharing

Review iOS notification permissions and device settings if reminders do not appear, and reopen the app to reconcile upcoming reminders. Calendar export is optional and uses generic titles. Calendar accounts and chosen sharing/printing destinations may store or sync their copies outside the app.

To share a profile, open Settings → Backup & Export → Share Profile. Choose the family member and follow the prompts to send the .medtimeline file through your preferred app.

To import a received profile, open its .medtimeline file in Medical Timeline, review the summary and confirm the import. This creates a new, independent profile; later edits do not sync. Readable PDF/ZIP exports are for reading and sharing outside the app; use .medtimeline files for backup restoration or profile import.

Report a technical problem safely

Email the developer with the app version from Settings → About, iOS version, iPhone model, what you tried, what happened and reproducible steps using fictional examples. Do not send medical documents, patient names, family photos, private backups or screenshots containing health information. Describe the control or error category without including private content. Support provides app assistance, not clinical advice or emergency care.
