Medical Timeline (listed in Hebrew as מה קבעתי) helps organize personal and family medical information. It does not verify, diagnose or interpret medical information, provide treatment recommendations or replace professional care. You choose whose information to enter. Make sure you have appropriate permission to record or share another person's information, including a child's information.

Information stored on your device

No account is required. The app stores names, optional birth dates and health coverage details, family relationships, profile photos, medical events, healthcare locations, doctors, notes, body-area labels, preparation tasks, documents, recognized text and reminder settings in its private local storage. Language and App Lock choices are local settings.

The developer operates no medical-data cloud, account server, advertising, analytics or tracking service in the app. The app does not automatically upload medical records to the developer. Apple purchases and external actions you choose are described separately below.

Choosing or taking a profile photo creates a local normalized copy. Scanning or importing document images creates a local PDF. Imported PDFs keep their original bytes and may contain original metadata. The source photo or file stays under the control of Photos, Files or its provider. On-device text recognition uses Apple Vision to assist document search; supported languages and accuracy vary. It does not medically interpret the document.

Protection and its limits

On iOS the app uses private storage and device file protection. Sensitive app files are excluded from ordinary operating-system app backup. The database is not separately encrypted with your backup password. Optional App Lock uses device-owner authentication, including device-passcode fallback; the app receives no biometric templates. Someone who knows your device passcode may authenticate. App-switcher previews are covered even when App Lock is off.

These protections do not guarantee absolute security, forensic erasure, protection on a compromised or unlocked device, or prevention of screenshots. Files and in-memory copies can remain temporarily after an interruption; cleanup may wait until a later successful app launch. External copies are outside App Lock's control.

Manual encrypted backups and restoration

You can create a password-encrypted full backup and explicitly save or share it. Backups are not automatic. Creating or checking a backup does not mean that you saved it outside this device. Choose a strong password and keep it safely: the app does not retain the backup password and the developer cannot recover it.

Full restoration replaces all current family and medical records in the app with the backup's records. It retains the destination device's local App Lock, onboarding and language choices. Reminder intentions are restored and reconciled locally; Calendar links and the old operating-system notification queue are not transferred. Existing Calendar copies or previously exported files are not removed by restoration. Purchase ownership is not part of a medical backup.

Device loss, damage or uninstalling the app can lose records unless you have saved an external backup and retained its password. Ordinary OS app backup is not a substitute for the app's manual encrypted backup.

Individual profiles and readable exports

An encrypted individual profile transfer contains a copy of the chosen person's records and documents. It omits relatives, reminders and Calendar links. The recipient needs the password; communicate it separately. Import creates an independent profile, not live synchronization. The receiving device chooses its own App Lock settings.

Readable medical-record export creates a PDF summary and original documents in a ZIP. Individual PDF sharing and printing are also explicit actions. These readable files are not password-encrypted by the app and cannot be used for full restoration. Anyone able to open them may read the information. Original documents may retain metadata. Select destinations and recipients carefully.

Files, Photos, AirDrop, mail, print and other destinations you choose may retrieve, store, transmit or synchronize information under their own policies, including cloud-backed providers. The app cannot revoke or delete their copies. Sending a password gives access to the corresponding encrypted file. The developer does not receive exports through an app backend.

Local reminders and Calendar

Reminders are scheduled locally. Notification titles and bodies are generic; the operating system receives schedules and opaque record identifiers, not medical titles or document text. Notifications depend on permissions and device settings. Only a bounded set of upcoming reminders is queued; opening the app reconciles later reminders. They are not guaranteed delivery or a substitute for care arrangements.

Calendar export happens only when you choose it. It creates a generic appointment or preparation title with a date/time. Calendar access lets the app inspect, update or delete a linked copy when requested; unrelated events are not imported into medical records. Calendar accounts may synchronize through their providers. Deleting a person, restoring a backup or removing a local link does not necessarily delete the external Calendar event.

Apple-managed purchases

Full Unlock is an optional one-time non-consumable purchase handled by Apple. StoreKit communicates with Apple for product pricing, purchase processing and verified purchase restoration. Connectivity and Apple Account authentication may be required. The app does not receive payment-card details or send medical profiles, records or documents with purchase requests. There is no developer purchase server or purchase analytics SDK. Apple handles its services under its own privacy practices. Encrypted full backup and restore remain free. Existing records remain accessible independently of purchase status.

Retention, deletion and support

Local records remain until you edit or delete them, replace them with a full restore, or remove the app. Deleting a person removes their owned records and documents. Deleting an event keeps its documents as standalone documents. Unreferenced and staging files are cleaned after use or at a later successful launch; failed IO or interruptions may delay cleanup. Deletion does not promise secure erasure. External files, source Photos/Files assets, Calendar copies and provider backups must be managed separately with those services.

Contact staroldur@gmail.com for app support. The contact action opens your mail app with the app version in the subject and an empty body; it attaches no medical data. If you choose to send email, your email service and the support mailbox handle what you send. Do not send medical documents, patient details or backup passwords.

Support mailbox provider, access, retention/deletion, international handling and applicable privacy-rights procedures await operator confirmation and external review. This draft does not establish a finalized request procedure.

This website and external services

This companion website is prepared for hosting on GitHub Pages. Visiting it sends technical requests to GitHub, which can process information such as IP addresses and request details under its own hosting and privacy practices. This differs from the app’s local medical storage. The developer has added no analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, cookies, forms, user accounts or external font services to these pages. Images and fonts are served with the website. Following an external link or sending support email involves the selected provider; its policies apply. The app’s manual choice of cloud-backed files and export destinations does not create a developer-operated medical cloud.

GitHub’s handling is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The operator must confirm hosting configuration and actual support practices before this draft becomes effective.

Policy changes

The operator will update this policy when app behavior or applicable requirements change and identify the effective date. A future remote-data feature would require separate product approval and an updated policy and App Privacy review.
