Medical Timeline (listed in Hebrew as מה קבעתי) helps organize personal and family medical information. It does not verify, diagnose or interpret medical information, provide treatment recommendations or replace professional care. You choose whose information to enter. Make sure you have appropriate permission to record or share another person's information, including a child's information.

Information stored on your device

No account is required. The app stores names, optional birth dates and health coverage details, family relationships, profile photos, medical events, healthcare locations, doctors, notes, body-area labels, preparation tasks, documents, recognized text and reminder settings in its private local storage. Language and App Lock choices are local settings.

The developer operates no medical-data cloud, account server, advertising, analytics or tracking service in the app. The app does not automatically upload medical records to the developer. Apple purchases and external actions you choose are described separately below.

Choosing or taking a profile photo creates a local normalized copy. Manual scans, automatic scans and imported document images are saved as local PDFs. Imported PDFs keep their original bytes and may contain original metadata. The source photo or file stays under the control of Photos, Files or its provider. On-device text recognition uses Apple Vision to assist document search; supported languages and accuracy vary. It does not medically interpret the document.

Protection and its limits

On iOS the app uses private storage and device file protection. Sensitive app files are excluded from ordinary operating-system app backup. The app relies on operating-system file protection for its private database; it does not manage a separate database encryption key. Optional App Lock uses device-owner authentication, including device-passcode fallback; the app receives no biometric templates. Someone who knows your device passcode may authenticate. App-switcher previews are covered even when App Lock is off.

These protections do not guarantee absolute security, forensic erasure, protection on a compromised or unlocked device, or prevention of screenshots. Files and in-memory copies can remain temporarily after an interruption; cleanup may wait until a later successful app launch. External copies are outside App Lock's control.

Manual backups and restoration

You can create a full backup without creating or entering a password and explicitly save or share it. Backups are manual, unencrypted ZIP-based .medtimeline files. Anyone with access to a backup can read the information, including medical records, documents and profile photos. Keep files in a safe place and share only with people you trust. Creating or checking a backup does not mean it was saved outside this device. There is no automatic medical-data backup or sync.

Full restoration replaces all current family and medical records in the app with the backup's records. It retains the destination device's local App Lock, onboarding and language choices. Reminder intentions are restored and reconciled locally; Calendar links and the old operating-system notification queue are not transferred. Existing Calendar copies or previously exported files are not removed by restoration. Purchase ownership is not part of a medical backup.

Device loss, damage or uninstalling the app can lose records unless you have saved an external backup. Ordinary OS app backup is not a substitute for the app's manual backup.

Files from older pre-release versions cannot be opened by this version. Existing on-device records are preserved through the update and can be backed up again. Keep old backup files; the app does not automatically delete or convert them.

Individual profiles and readable exports

A shared profile is an unencrypted ZIP-based .medtimeline file containing an independent copy of the chosen person's records, Medical Profile conditions, documents and photo. No password is required. Anyone with access to the file can read its contents. It omits relatives, reminders and Calendar links. The recipient reviews and confirms import, which creates a new profile and does not overwrite an existing person or synchronize later edits. The receiving device chooses its own App Lock settings.

Readable medical-record export creates a PDF summary and original documents in a ZIP. Individual PDF sharing and printing are also explicit actions. These files are unencrypted and cannot be used for full restoration. Anyone able to open them may read the information. Original documents may retain metadata. Select destinations and recipients carefully.

Files, Photos, AirDrop, mail, print and other destinations you choose may retrieve, store, transmit or synchronize information under their own policies, including cloud-backed providers. The app cannot revoke or delete their copies. The developer does not receive exports through an app backend.

Local reminders and Calendar

Reminders are scheduled locally. Notification titles and bodies are generic; the operating system receives schedules and opaque record identifiers, not medical titles or document text. Notifications depend on permissions and device settings. Only a bounded set of upcoming reminders is queued; opening the app reconciles later reminders. They are not guaranteed delivery or a substitute for care arrangements.

Calendar export happens only when you choose it. It creates a generic appointment or preparation title with a date/time. Calendar access lets the app inspect, update or delete a linked copy when requested; unrelated events are not imported into medical records. Calendar accounts may synchronize through their providers. Deleting a person, restoring a backup or removing a local link does not necessarily delete the external Calendar event.

Apple-managed purchases

Full Unlock is an optional one-time non-consumable purchase handled by Apple. StoreKit communicates with Apple for product pricing, purchase processing and verified purchase restoration. Connectivity and Apple Account authentication may be required. The app does not receive payment-card details or send medical profiles, records or documents with purchase requests. There is no developer purchase server or purchase analytics SDK. Apple handles its services under its own privacy practices. Full backup and restore remain free. Existing records remain accessible independently of purchase status.

Local retention and deletion

Local records remain until you edit or delete them, replace them with a full restore, or remove the app. Deleting a person removes their owned records and documents. Deleting an event keeps its documents as standalone documents. Unreferenced and staging files are cleaned after use or at a later successful launch; failed IO or interruptions may delay cleanup. Deletion does not promise secure erasure. External files, source Photos/Files assets, Calendar copies and provider backups must be managed separately with those services.

Voluntary support correspondence

Contact Stas Stern at staroldur@gmail.com for app support or privacy questions. Contacting support is optional and is not required to use the app. No support email is sent automatically. The app’s contact action opens your email application with the app version in the subject, an empty body and no medical attachments; you decide whether to send. Website email links also open your chosen email application.

The developer receives the correspondence you voluntarily send, which may include your sender address, subject, message content and any attachments you choose to include. It is used to answer questions, investigate technical problems, follow up on your inquiry and handle privacy requests. You do not have to provide medical information to receive technical support; without enough non-sensitive details, it may not be possible to investigate a particular problem. Please do not send medical information, patient names, medical documents, family photos, or private backups. If you nevertheless include sensitive information, it may be received as part of your email; this does not create a developer-operated medical-data server.

The support mailbox is a Gmail address operated through Google’s email services. On the developer’s side, mailbox access is limited to Stas Stern. Google, your own email provider and other providers involved in delivering email may process and store correspondence under their own policies. Their processing may take place outside your country, including outside Israel. The app does not automatically send medical records to Google or any other email provider.

Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably necessary to handle the inquiry, related follow-up and applicable obligations. There is no fixed automatic deletion period. You may request deletion of your correspondence at staroldur@gmail.com. Deletion may be limited by applicable obligations and email-provider retention or backups; permanent erasure of all provider copies cannot be guaranteed.

Privacy rights and requests

Stas Stern is an individual developer based in Israel. The app is intended for English-speaking international customers and Hebrew-speaking users in Israel. Availability in particular countries depends on App Store Connect settings; availability everywhere is not promised. Applicable privacy rights may vary by jurisdiction. This policy does not limit rights provided by applicable law.

Local medical data: the developer has no remote copy or account system through which to retrieve, correct or delete your on-device records. Use the app to review, edit or delete local information, or remove the app from the device. Manage backups saved elsewhere, source documents and previously shared copies separately with the storage providers or recipients you selected. Sending an email request does not remotely delete local records or those external copies.

Support correspondence: for questions or requests concerning personal information in support messages, email staroldur@gmail.com. You may request access to, correction of or deletion of that information where provided by applicable law. Describe the request and, where possible, send it from the address used for the correspondence. The developer may need reasonable verification before disclosing, correcting or deleting correspondence. Do not send medical documents or identity-document copies with the initial request. Requests will be handled according to applicable law; no additional fixed response deadline is promised here.

Permissions and external records

You can manage camera, Photos, Calendar, notifications and other device permissions through iOS Settings, as applicable. Some selections use the system’s file or photo picker rather than broad library access. Withdrawing a permission may prevent the related feature from working and does not by itself delete information already saved.

External Calendar copies, cloud documents, backups and readable shared files may require separate action with the respective providers or recipients. Apple manages purchase history and Apple Account records under its own practices. Medical Timeline cannot erase Apple’s records or revoke copies held by other services.

This website and external services

This companion website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting it sends technical requests to GitHub. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security and may process request details under its own hosting and privacy practices. This is separate from the app’s local medical storage. The developer has added no analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, cookies, forms, user accounts or external font services to these pages. Images and fonts are served with the website.

Following an external link or choosing to use an email, file, Calendar, sharing or purchase service involves that provider and its policies. Google handles Gmail correspondence, GitHub handles website hosting and Apple handles its purchase and platform services. These roles do not create a developer-operated medical cloud. The app does not automatically transmit its local medical records to the website or the support mailbox.

Policy changes

The operator will update this policy when app behavior or applicable requirements change and identify the effective date. A future remote-data feature would require separate product approval and an updated policy and App Privacy review.
