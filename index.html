A LITTLE ORDER, EVERY DAY

The details, connected.

Start with a personal profile. Full Unlock brings family members, their relationships and a shared view of the family agenda into one local organizer.

01 / KEEP THE CONTEXT

Every appointment has a place.

Keep past records and upcoming appointments on a personal medical timeline. Add locations, notes and body-area labels to find your way back.

02 / LESS PAPERWORK

From a paper document to a record.

Scan at your own pace with the manual camera, or choose automatic scanning. Review pages, import PDFs and search titles or recognized text. On-device text recognition varies by language and document.

03 / READY FOR THE NEXT STEP

Make room for preparation.

Add preparation tasks and local reminders. Choose to add a generic appointment copy to Calendar. Reminders depend on device settings; the app does not book appointments.

A PRIVATE FOUNDATION

Close to you.
Stored on your device.

Medical information stays in private local storage. Optional App Lock uses device-owner authentication. There are no developer accounts, analytics or automatic medical uploads.

Create a password-encrypted backup and explicitly save it outside the device. Backups are manual; losing the device or uninstalling can lose records. Keep the file and its password safe.
